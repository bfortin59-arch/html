Station Five – Central Area

This open space formed the heart of the fort. Soldiers gathered here for roll call, drill, and daily routines. The area also served as a staging point for work crews assigned to maintain the walls and road.

During the winter of 1861–62, tents and makeshift shelters filled this area as the garrison struggled against cold and disease.
