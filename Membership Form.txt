  <section class="content">

    <h2>Why Membership Matters</h2>
    <p>
      Fort Duffield stands today because generations of volunteers refused to let it fade into the hillside. 
      Becoming a member of the Friends of Fort Duffield connects you to that legacy. Your support helps maintain 
      the fort’s earthworks, preserve the memorial cemetery, fund educational programs, and keep this historic 
      site open to the public.
    </p>

    <div class="callout">
      Membership is one of the most direct ways to protect the fort, honor the soldiers who built it, and ensure 
      that future generations can walk these grounds and understand their story.
    </div>

    <h2>Membership Levels</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Individual – $20/year</strong>  
        Supports general maintenance and preservation.</li>
      <li><strong>Family – $35/year</strong>  
        Ideal for households who enjoy hiking, events, and volunteer days.</li>
      <li><strong>Supporting – $75/year</strong>  
        Helps fund interpretive signage, school programs, and research.</li>
      <li><strong>Patron – $150/year</strong>  
        Provides major support for restoration projects and trail upkeep.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>How to Join</h2>
    <p>
      Membership is simple. Download the form below, complete your information, and mail it with your dues to:
    </p>

    <p><strong>Friends of Fort Duffield  
    P.O. Box 82  
    West Point, KY 40177</strong></p>

    <p>
      You may also bring your completed form to any volunteer day or event at the fort.
    </p>
