Fort Duffield stands today because people choose to protect it. Every trail cleared, every sign installed, every school group welcomed — it all depends on community support. Your contribution helps preserve one of Kentucky’s most important Civil War sites for future generations.

Why Your Donation Matters

  • Maintains historic earthworks and trails
  • Supports cemetery preservation
  • Funds educational programs for kids and schools
  • Provides materials for signage and interpretation
  • Helps host community events and volunteer days

How to Give

Donations to the Fort Duffield Heritage Committee directly support preservation and public education.

  • One-time donations
  • Monthly support
  • Sponsorship of specific projects
  • Memorial or honor gifts

All funds stay local and benefit the site directly.

What Your Gift Supports

Preservation & Maintenance

Tools, materials, and supplies needed to care for the fort, trails, and cemetery.

Education & Interpretation

Maps, signage, school packets, and public programming.

Community Engagement

Events, volunteer coordination, and outreach efforts.

A Legacy Worth Preserving

Fort Duffield is a rare survivor — a hillside fort built by young soldiers in the earliest days of the Civil War. Your support ensures their story continues to be told with accuracy, dignity, and care.
