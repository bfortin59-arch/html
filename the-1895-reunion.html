The 1895 Reunion

In 1895, veterans who had served at Fort Duffield returned to West Point to reunite, reminisce, and honor the memory of their fallen comrades. The gathering reflected the enduring bonds formed during the difficult winter of 1861–62.

Survivors shared stories of building the fort, enduring sickness, and guarding the vital crossroads at the mouth of the Salt River. Their reunion helped preserve the fort’s history and ensured that future generations would remember their service.

This page commemorates that important event and the legacy of the men who returned to the hillside where they once stood as young soldiers.
