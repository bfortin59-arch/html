    <h2>Camping at a Historic Site</h2>
    <p>
      Fort Duffield offers a rare opportunity for scout troops, reenactors, and educational groups to camp 
      inside a preserved Civil War landscape. The quiet ridgeline, the deep woods, and the earthworks 
      themselves create an atmosphere that is both peaceful and deeply connected to the past. 
    </p>

    <p>
      Camping here is more than an overnight stay — it is a chance to experience the terrain as the soldiers 
      once did. The steep climb, the commanding overlook of West Point, and the isolation of the ridge 
      help visitors understand why the Union Army chose this site and what life was like for the men who 
      built and defended the fort.
    </p>

    <div class="callout">
      Overnight camping helps support the fort’s mission by encouraging outdoor education, historical 
      awareness, and responsible stewardship of the land.
    </div>

    <h2>Who Can Camp at Fort Duffield</h2>
    <p>
      Camping is available to:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Scout troops (BSA, Girl Scouts, Trail Life, etc.)</li>
      <li>School groups and educational programs</li>
      <li>Historical reenactment units</li>
      <li>Veterans’ groups and heritage organizations</li>
      <li>Other community groups with prior approval</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>What to Expect</h2>
    <p>
      The camping area is rustic and intentionally preserved to maintain the historic character of the site. 
      Groups should be prepared for:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Primitive camping conditions</li>
      <li>No electricity or running water at the campsite</li>
      <li>Pack‑in/pack‑out expectations</li>
      <li>Respect for earthworks, trails, and memorial areas</li>
      <li>Quiet hours to preserve the natural setting</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Why Forms Are Required</h2>
    <p>
      Because Fort Duffield is a historic site maintained entirely by volunteers, camping forms help ensure 
      that groups understand the rules, safety expectations, and preservation guidelines. These forms also 
      help the Friends of Fort Duffield coordinate schedules, prepare the site, and provide support when needed.
    </p>
