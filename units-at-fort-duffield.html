Units at Fort Duffield

Several Union regiments served at Fort Duffield during its brief but intense period of occupation. The two primary units were:

9th Michigan Infantry

Arrived in late 1861 and endured the harsh winter that claimed many lives. Their labor built much of the fort’s structure.

37th Indiana Infantry

Assisted in construction and manned the fort during the same winter. Disease and exposure took a heavy toll on the regiment.

Other smaller detachments rotated through the fort as the Union Army secured the region.
