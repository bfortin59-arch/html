Memorial Cemetery

The Fort Duffield Memorial Cemetery is the final resting place of Union soldiers who died during the winter of 1861–62. Disease, exposure, and harsh conditions claimed many lives, and the cemetery stands today as a solemn reminder of their sacrifice.

Each headstone represents a story—young men far from home, serving their country during one of the most difficult winters of the Civil War. Volunteers and community members have worked for decades to preserve this sacred ground.

Visiting the Cemetery

  • Please respect all markers and fencing.
  • Stay on designated paths.
  • Photography is welcome.
  • Quiet reflection is encouraged.

The cemetery remains one of the most meaningful places at Fort Duffield, honoring those who never returned home.
