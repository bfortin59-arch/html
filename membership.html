Become a Member

Membership in the Fort Duffield Heritage Committee helps preserve one of Kentucky’s most important Civil War sites. Your support strengthens education, restoration, and community engagement.

Membership Benefits

  • Support preservation of the fort and cemetery
  • Receive updates on projects and events
  • Invitation to member-only volunteer days
  • Recognition on our website (optional)
  • Help shape the future of Fort Duffield

Membership Levels

  • Individual Member – Annual support
  • Family Member – Support for households
  • Supporting Member – Helps fund special projects
  • Lifetime Member – One-time contribution

How to Join

Memberships can be completed by mail or during volunteer events. Online membership options will be added soon.
