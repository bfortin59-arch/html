Stand Up for Jesus

“Stand Up for Jesus” was one of the hymns sung by Union soldiers during the Civil War, including those stationed at Fort Duffield. Hymns and spiritual songs provided comfort, unity, and a sense of purpose during long nights in camp.

While the hymn predates the war, its message resonated deeply with soldiers facing hardship, uncertainty, and separation from home. Music became a vital part of camp life, echoing across the hills of West Point during the winter of 1861–62.

This page explores the role of faith, music, and morale among the men who built and defended Fort Duffield.
