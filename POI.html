Points of Interest

Fort Duffield offers several notable locations for visitors to explore. Each point of interest provides insight into the fort’s construction, purpose, and the daily life of the soldiers stationed here.

  • The Earthworks: Well-preserved defensive walls built by Union soldiers.
  • The Cemetery: Resting place of soldiers who died during the winter of 1861–62.
  • The Overlook: A commanding view of the Salt River valley and railroad.
  • The Spring: A vital water source for the garrison.
  • Memorial Plaza: A modern tribute to the fort’s history and preservation.

Visitors are encouraged to explore these areas respectfully and stay on marked trails.
