A Site with Stories

Fort Duffield’s history of hardship, illness, and sacrifice has led to decades of reported paranormal experiences. Visitors, volunteers, and investigators have shared stories of unexplained sounds, shadows, and sensations.

Common Reports

  • Footsteps along the earthworks
  • Voices or whispers near the cemetery
  • Cold spots on warm days
  • Shadow figures along the ridge
  • Unusual activity during nighttime investigations

Respect the Site

Whether you visit for history or paranormal interest, please treat the fort and cemetery with respect. This is hallowed ground where young soldiers lived, suffered, and died.

Paranormal Groups

Investigators may request permission for after-hours access. Contact the Heritage Committee for guidelines.
