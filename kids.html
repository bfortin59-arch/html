For Young Explorers

Fort Duffield is a great place for kids to explore, learn, and imagine what life was like for young soldiers during the Civil War.

Activities

Scavenger Hunt

Kids can look for:

  • A bend in the earthworks
  • A view of the river
  • A trail sign
  • A historic marker
  • A quiet spot to reflect

Vocabulary List

  • Earthworks: Walls made of dirt for protection
  • Fort: A place built to defend an area
  • Regiment: A large group of soldiers
  • Supply line: The route that brings food and equipment to soldiers

“Build a Fort” Activity

In the classroom or at home, kids can design their own fort on paper — choosing where to put walls, lookouts, and shelters, and thinking about how the land helps protect it.

Before and After the Visit

  • Before: Talk about what a fort is and why soldiers needed one at Fort Duffield.
  • After: Ask kids what surprised them most and what they remember about the soldiers’ lives.
