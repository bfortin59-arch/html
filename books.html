Books

This page highlights books and published works that explore Fort Duffield, the Civil War in Kentucky, and the experiences of the soldiers who served here. These resources provide deeper insight into the fort’s construction, the hardships of camp life, and the broader military context of 1861–1862.

Recommended Reading

  • Local and Regional Histories – Works detailing the military significance of West Point and the L&N Railroad.
  • Regimental Histories – Publications covering the 9th Michigan, 37th Indiana, and other units connected to the fort.
  • Soldier Memoirs and Diaries – Firsthand accounts that illuminate daily life, hardship, and duty.

Additional titles will be added as research continues and new materials become available.
