Rules & Requirements

  • All fires must be contained and monitored.
  • No modern trash left on site.
  • Respect all historic earthworks and marked areas.
  • Follow all instructions from Fort Duffield staff and volunteers.
  • Restrooms and water access may be limited depending on season.

Submit completed forms to the Fort Duffield Heritage Committee for approval.
