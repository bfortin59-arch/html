Memorial Plaza

The Memorial Plaza honors the soldiers who served at Fort Duffield and the volunteers who have worked to preserve the site. The plaza serves as a gathering place for ceremonies, educational programs, and moments of reflection.

Features

  • Memorial stones and plaques
  • Flag displays
  • Benches for quiet reflection
  • Interpretive signage

The plaza stands as a testament to the community’s dedication to preserving the memory of those who served on the ridge.
