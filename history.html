A Fort Built in Urgency. A Story That Endures.

High above West Point, overlooking the Salt and Ohio Rivers, Fort Duffield tells a story of strategy, hardship, and determination. Built in the winter of 1861–62 by young Union soldiers, the fort protected Louisville and the vital supply line that kept the Union Army moving.

This page introduces the people, decisions, and events that shaped the fort’s creation — and its legacy.

Why This History Matters

Fort Duffield is more than an earthwork. It is a story of young Americans facing war, illness, and uncertainty — and of a community today committed to preserving their memory.
