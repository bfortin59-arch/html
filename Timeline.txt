  <header class="page-header">
    <h1>Timeline 1861–1862</h1>
    <p>A chronological look at the events surrounding Fort Duffield’s construction and early occupation.</p>
  </header>

  <section class="content">

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">September 1861</div>
      Union forces arrive in West Point to secure the river landing and supply route.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">October 1861</div>
      Soldiers begin cutting the military road up the ridge.  
      Work starts on the fort’s earthworks.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">November 1861</div>
      Construction intensifies.  
      The 9th Michigan Infantry takes the lead in shaping the walls and ditches.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">December 1861</div>
      Winter sets in.  
      Disease spreads rapidly through the camp.  
      The Memorial Cemetery begins to fill.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">January 1862</div>
      The fort nears completion.  
      Artillery positions are prepared along the ridge.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">February 1862</div>
      Union forces shift southward following victories at Fort Henry and Fort Donelson.  
      Fort Duffield’s strategic importance begins to decline.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">Spring 1862</div>
      Most troops leave West Point.  
      The fort remains as a defensive fallback position but is no longer heavily manned.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">Summer 1862</div>
      Guerrilla activity increases in the region.  
      Small detachments continue to monitor the supply line.
    </div>

    <div class="timeline-entry">
      <div class="timeline-date">Late 1862</div>
      The fort is largely abandoned as the war shifts deeper into the South.
    </div>

  </section>
