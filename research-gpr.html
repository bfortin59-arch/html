Ground Penetrating Radar

Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) has become an essential tool in understanding the hidden features of Fort Duffield. This non-invasive technology allows researchers to detect subsurface structures without disturbing the historic landscape.

What GPR Reveals

  • Possible locations of buried features such as hut sites or storage pits
  • Subsurface soil disturbances related to fort construction
  • Potential unmarked graves or earlier burial locations
  • Changes in soil composition that indicate historic activity

Why It Matters

GPR helps guide preservation decisions and ensures that sensitive areas are protected. By identifying what lies beneath the surface, researchers can better interpret the daily life of soldiers and the layout of the fort during the winter of 1861–62.

Ongoing Research

Additional GPR surveys are planned as part of long-term preservation efforts. Each scan contributes to a clearer picture of Fort Duffield’s past and helps inform future restoration and educational projects.
