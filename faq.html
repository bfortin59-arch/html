Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fort Duffield open year-round?

Yes. The site is open daily from dawn to dusk.

Is there an admission fee?

No. Fort Duffield is free to visit.

Are guided tours available?

Guided tours may be offered during special events. Self-guided tours are available anytime.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, but pets must be kept on a leash.

Is the site suitable for children?

Yes. Children should be supervised due to steep terrain.

Where can I park?

Parking is available near the entrance at the base of the ridge.
