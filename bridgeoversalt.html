Bridge Over Salt River

The Salt River bridge was a critical link in the Louisville & Nashville Railroad system. Protecting this bridge was one of the primary reasons Fort Duffield was constructed.

Union engineers and soldiers worked tirelessly to maintain the bridge, which carried troops, supplies, and communication between Louisville and the advancing armies in Tennessee.

The fort’s commanding view of the river ensured that any attempt to sabotage the bridge could be quickly detected and countered.
