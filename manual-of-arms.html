Skirmish in Town

Although Fort Duffield itself never came under direct attack, a brief skirmish occurred in the town of West Point. Confederate cavalry probed the area, testing Union defenses and attempting to disrupt supply routes.

Union pickets and local Home Guard units responded quickly, driving off the attackers. The incident reinforced the importance of the fort’s elevated position and the need for constant vigilance.

The skirmish remains one of the few recorded combat-related events connected to Fort Duffield.
