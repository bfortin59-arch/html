History of Fort Duffield

Fort Duffield was constructed in late 1861 to protect the vital transportation routes at West Point, Kentucky. Built by Union soldiers under harsh winter conditions, the fort played a key role in securing the Louisville & Nashville Railroad and the Salt River crossing.
