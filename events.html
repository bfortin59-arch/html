Events at Fort Duffield

Fort Duffield hosts a variety of community events throughout the year, including living history programs, memorial ceremonies, volunteer workdays, and educational activities. These events help preserve the fort’s legacy and connect visitors with the stories of the soldiers who served here.

Annual Events

  • Memorial Day Ceremony – A solemn tribute to the soldiers buried in the Fort Duffield Cemetery.
  • Veterans Day Observance – Honoring all who have served, past and present.
  • Living History Weekends – Reenactors and historians bring the Civil War era to life.
  • Volunteer Workdays – Community members help maintain trails, clear brush, and preserve the fort.

Special Programs

Throughout the year, Fort Duffield may host guided tours, educational presentations, archaeological demonstrations, and historical talks. Check back often for updates.
