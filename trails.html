Trails

Fort Duffield features several hiking trails that guide visitors through the historic landscape. These trails offer scenic views, access to key features, and opportunities to experience the ridge as soldiers once did.

Main Trails

  • Ridge Trail: Follows the top of the ridge and connects major fort features.
  • Spring Trail: Leads to the natural spring used by soldiers.
  • Overlook Trail: Offers panoramic views of the Salt River valley.

Trail Guidelines

  • Stay on marked paths.
  • Wear appropriate footwear.
  • Carry water, especially in warm weather.
  • Respect wildlife and natural features.

Enjoy your hike and take time to appreciate the history beneath your feet.
