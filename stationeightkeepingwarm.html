Station Eight – Keeping Warm

Surviving the winter of 1861–62 was one of the greatest challenges faced by the soldiers. They built crude log huts, dug fire pits, and used whatever materials they could find to insulate their shelters.

Despite their efforts, cold and disease claimed many lives—making this station a reminder of the hardships endured on the ridge.
