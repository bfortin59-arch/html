Timeline: 1861–1862

  • September 1861: Union forces secure West Point as a supply hub.
  • October 1861: Construction of Fort Duffield begins on the ridge.
  • November 1861: The 9th Michigan and 37th Indiana arrive to garrison the fort.
  • Winter 1861–62: Disease and exposure take a heavy toll on the soldiers.
  • February 1862: Union victories at Fort Henry and Fort Donelson shift the war south.
  • Spring 1862: Fort Duffield is gradually abandoned as troops move to new fronts.

This timeline highlights the brief but intense period during which Fort Duffield played a vital defensive role in Kentucky.
