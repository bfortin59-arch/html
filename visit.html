Fort Duffield offers a quiet, meaningful experience for families, hikers, school groups, and history enthusiasts. The fort sits high above West Point, with wooded trails, preserved earthworks, and sweeping views of the Salt and Ohio Rivers.

Location

Fort Duffield Park
Fort Duffield Road
West Point, Kentucky

The entrance is located just above the town, near the base of Muldraugh Hill. Parking is available near the trailhead.

Hours & Admission

The park is open year-round from dawn to dusk.

There is no admission fee.

What You’ll See

  • Earthwork fortifications
  • Fort Duffield Cemetery
  • Scenic overlooks of the rivers and West Point
  • Interpretive signs and historical points of interest
  • Wooded hiking trails

The site is rustic and natural. Wear comfortable shoes and be prepared for uneven ground.

Trails

  • Main Trail: Moderate incline, leads directly to the fort
  • Cemetery Trail: Short spur to the soldiers’ resting place
  • Overlook Trail: Views of the Salt River and Ohio River
  • Extended Trails: Additional wooded paths for longer hikes

Trails are maintained by volunteers and may vary with weather conditions.

Accessibility

Because the fort sits on a steep hillside, some areas may be challenging for visitors with mobility limitations. The Heritage Committee continues to improve access where possible.

Visitor Tips

  • Bring water, especially in warm months
  • Wear sturdy footwear
  • Stay on marked trails
  • Respect the cemetery and historic features
  • Leave no trace
