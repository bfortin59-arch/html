Join the Effort to Preserve Fort Duffield

Fort Duffield survives because people care. Every trail cleared, every sign repaired, every school group welcomed — it all happens through the dedication of volunteers. If you love history, the outdoors, or community service, there’s a place for you here.

Why Volunteer?

  • Protect one of Kentucky’s most important Civil War sites
  • Help maintain trails, earthworks, and the historic cemetery
  • Support educational programs for kids and visitors
  • Build community with people who share your passion
  • Make a lasting impact on a site that tells a powerful story

Ways to Get Involved

Trail & Grounds Maintenance

Help keep the fort accessible by clearing brush, repairing paths, and maintaining signage.

Cemetery Care

Assist with respectful upkeep of the soldiers’ resting place.

Educational Programs

Support school visits, lead tours, or help develop hands-on activities.

Special Events

Volunteer during living-history days, community clean-ups, and commemorations.

Research & Documentation

Help uncover new historical details, preserve local stories, and support interpretive projects.

Who Can Volunteer?

Individuals, families, scout troops, school groups, and community organizations are all welcome. No prior experience is required — just enthusiasm and a willingness to help.

What to Expect

  • Most work is outdoors
  • Tools and guidance are provided
  • Projects vary by season
  • Volunteers work at their own pace
  • All ages and skill levels can contribute
