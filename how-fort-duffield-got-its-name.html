How Fort Duffield Got Its Name

Fort Duffield was named in honor of Colonel William W. Duffield of the 9th Michigan Infantry. Although he was wounded at Mill Springs and never commanded the fort directly, his leadership and service were respected by the men who built and occupied the ridge.

The naming also reflected the Union Army’s tradition of honoring officers who demonstrated courage and commitment early in the war.
