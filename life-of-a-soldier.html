The Life of a Soldier at West Point, KY

Daily life at Fort Duffield was defined by hard labor, cold weather, and constant vigilance. Soldiers spent long hours digging earthworks, hauling supplies, and maintaining the steep road up the ridge.

Food was limited, shelter was crude, and disease spread quickly. Despite the hardships, the men formed strong bonds and took pride in their work defending the region.
