Preserving History. Serving the Community.

The Fort Duffield Heritage Committee is a volunteer-driven organization dedicated to protecting, interpreting, and sharing the history of Fort Duffield. Our mission is to ensure that this remarkable Civil War site remains preserved, accessible, and meaningful for future generations.

Our Mission

  • Preserve the historic earthworks, cemetery, and surrounding landscape
  • Provide accurate, engaging historical interpretation
  • Support educational opportunities for schools and families
  • Maintain trails and improve visitor access
  • Host community events and volunteer days
  • Protect the legacy of the soldiers who built and served at Fort Duffield

Who We Are

The Committee is made up of local volunteers, historians, educators, veterans, and community supporters. Each person brings unique skills, but all share a commitment to honoring the fort’s story.

What We Do

Preservation & Maintenance

Regular workdays keep trails clear, signage updated, and historic features protected.

Education & Outreach

We develop school programs, guided tours, interpretive materials, and public events.

Research & Documentation

Ongoing research helps uncover new details about the fort, the soldiers, and the Civil War in Kentucky.

Community Engagement

We partner with local organizations, host commemorations, and encourage public involvement.

How You Can Help

  • Volunteer your time
  • Attend events
  • Share local history
  • Support preservation efforts
  • Bring your family or group to visit

Fort Duffield survives because people choose to be part of its story.
