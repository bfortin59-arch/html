Upkeep – Goings On Here

Fort Duffield is maintained entirely by volunteers who dedicate their time to preserving the fort, cemetery, trails, and memorial areas. Their work ensures that visitors can safely explore the site and learn from its history.

Recent Projects

  • Trail clearing and erosion control
  • Fence and marker repairs in the cemetery
  • Brush removal around earthworks
  • Interpretive sign updates and replacements
  • Seasonal mowing and grounds maintenance

How You Can Help

Volunteers are always welcome. Whether you can give an hour or a full day, your help makes a difference. Tasks include clearing brush, maintaining trails, repairing signage, and assisting with events.

Contact the Fort Duffield Heritage Committee to learn about upcoming workdays and volunteer opportunities.
