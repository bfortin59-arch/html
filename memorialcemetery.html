A Place of Reflection

The Memorial Plaza honors the soldiers who built and served at Fort Duffield. It serves as a quiet space for reflection, remembrance, and community gatherings.

Features of the Plaza

  • Memorial markers honoring Union soldiers
  • Interpretive signage
  • Benches and seating areas
  • Landscaped surroundings maintained by volunteers

Events at the Plaza

  • Memorial Day ceremonies
  • Veterans’ events
  • Historical presentations
  • Community gatherings

Support the Plaza

Donations help maintain the plaza, improve landscaping, and support future memorial features.
